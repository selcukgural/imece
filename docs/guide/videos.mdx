# Video tours

Four short tours, recorded from imece's real screen running the
[simulation](getting-started/demo.md), so they show the same screens you
see, at an even pace. The videos are in English; the captions can be turned
on in the player, and every chapter links to the page that explains it.

## 1 · What imece is

Home: what is to decide, then the team at work; an agent's detail and its
raw stream; a change opened with its verdict and applied; the pot; the
board, the code map and the timeline as windows; a line to the leader; the
quit question.
