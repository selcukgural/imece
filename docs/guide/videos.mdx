# Video tours

Three short tours, recorded from imece's real screen running the
[simulation](getting-started/demo.md), so they show the same screens you
see, at an even pace. The videos are in English; the captions can be turned
on in the player, and every chapter links to the page that explains it.

## 1 · What imece is

A leader, its workers and their helpers on one screen; a change that waits
for you, a command that waits for you, the pot and the limits, and the quit
question.
