# Video tours

Three short tours, each recorded from a **real** imece session: real Claude
Code agents working on a small Python project, real tests, a real Lonca
daemon. Nothing is simulated; the narration says what happened on the
screen. Every key pressed shows below the terminal with what it does, and
waits are sped up (the corner says how much). The videos are in English; the
captions can be turned on in the player, and every chapter links to the page
that explains it. Watched in order, they take you from installing imece to
a team at work; the [cheat sheet](cheat-sheet.md) has every key on one page.

## 1 · Your first change

`imece` in a checkout, the welcome, one task to the leader, a worker hired
for it, its change with the verdict on top, the diff, `a` to approve (the
change lands staged), `:commit` for a message, and quitting.
