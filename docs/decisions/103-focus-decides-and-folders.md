# A focus decides its agent's asks, and says where it works

The person, 2026-11-04 (a screenshot of a solo's focus): `a` on a focus
allowed something, but the footer named no such key; and asks kept
coming after `a`. A third wish the same night: to see which folder one
works in.

**What `a` did on a focus.** Nothing of its own: the key fell through
to waiting for you's `a`, which decides the item *selected there* when
that panel is the one at hand. A focus opened from waiting for you kept
that panel at hand, so `a` decided its selection: the first time the
focused agent's ask, after that whatever the selection moved to,
another agent's ask the person could not see. `A` fell through the same
way to a yes from now on for that other ask.

**Decided.** On a focus or a raw stream with no conversation over it
(`App::zoomed_agent`), `a`, `r` and `A` decide what *that* agent waits
for (`App::focus_ask`): its oldest open ask, the one its status quotes
(`World::open_item`'s order), else its change; never waiting for you's
selection (`App::zoom_answer_key`, before the focus's own keys).
`a`/`r` on an ask allow once / refuse (`App::answer_item`, the old
`answer` on an index), on a change apply / reject (`decide_change`; the
focus stays open). `A` on an ask is the yes from now on
(`App::answer_always_to`, record 89's words when it cannot be one); on a
change or with nothing waiting it opens the archives, as anywhere. With
nothing waiting `a` and `r` flash `NAME waits for no yes and no change
of yours`. The footer starts with `a allow once  A yes from now on  r
refuse` or `a apply  r reject` while there is one (`ui::ask_hints`, not
under a peek, hint letters or a script's window); waiting for you's row
says `a yes · A from now on · r no · enter focus` (it said only `a
allow`, so `A` was never seen, which is why the asks "kept coming").

**Why asks still repeat.** `a` is a yes for one call, by design. The
asks in the person's report could not have had a rule either:
`sqlite3 -readonly …` starts with an option and `gh issue list --repo
"$(git … )"` has a `$(…)`, both refused by record 89 on purpose. The person
chose a yes for one agent until its turn ends: record 104.

**The folder.** The focus has a `folder` line under the status
(`App::folder_of`): a leader works in the checkout, a worker, its
helpers and a solo in their worktree (`· worktree of ~/src/kule`), all
of them in the folder itself without git (`Live::folder_of`, the same
rule as `cwd_of` without the disk lookup, as the screen draws it). An
open project's salon line ends with its folder, dim and last, so it is
the first part a narrow panel cuts (`App::project_folder`); a folded one
ends with its loudest member as before. Paths are shown with `~` for the
home and through `clean`. The demo says the folder `:open` or `:solo`
was given (`Sim::folder_of`, kept at the open, by project), else
`~/src/NAME`, and worktrees under
`~/.local/state/imece/worktrees/NAME-5e1f0a2c/` as its `:tidy` does.

- **Read cost**: a frame reads one path per open project and one for the
  focus; nothing scans the journal.
- **Growth**: the demo keeps one string per project opened in a run.
